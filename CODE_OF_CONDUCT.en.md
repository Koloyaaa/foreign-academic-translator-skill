# Code of Conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | English

> This is a translation of [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The Chinese version is authoritative.

Be respectful and stay on the substance when you take part in discussions, open issues, or contribute code.

We welcome participants of any language, discipline, and experience level. Address the work and the evidence. Do not harass, humiliate, or threaten people. Do not publish anyone's private information, and do not put real course documents or account details into issues, discussions, or test samples.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close content that breaks this code, and may limit participation when needed. If you encounter misconduct, contact the repository maintainer privately.
